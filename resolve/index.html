Accelerate Incident Resolution

Smarter incident management, powered by AI

Smart Pre-submission Check

Before a new incident is created, Resolve's AI scans for similar open cases you can subscribe to, and surfaces related playbooks that may help you resolve the issue on your own — reducing ticket volume and getting you to a solution faster.

Automated Incident Triage

Once submitted, Resolve's AI automatically summarizes the incident, determines whether it's an incident or a service request, classifies it with the right tags, and assigns a priority based on severity and organizational context — so critical issues always get immediate attention.

Intelligent Routing & Assignment

Resolve automatically routes each incident to the most suitable team based on expertise, then assigns the right responder based on skills and current workload — eliminating manual handoffs and accelerating resolution.

Full visibility into your organization's incidents

Full visibility over your incidents

Track every incident from one live dashboard. Filter by team, priority, status, or type, and see active cases, resolution times, and volumes at a glance.

Spot recurring patterns automatically

Resolve groups similar incidents by vendor, root cause, location, or impact, and explains each cluster with AI, so your team fixes patterns, not just tickets.
